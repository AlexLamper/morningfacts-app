# MorningFacts Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

MorningFacts is made by Alex Lamper (AL Design, the Netherlands). This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## What stays on your phone

Your chosen language, topics, levels, likes and dislikes, saved facts, streak and reminder time are stored only on your device. MorningFacts has no accounts and does not send this information to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Purchases

Premium is sold through the App Store and Google Play. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your Premium subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app. See the [RevenueCat privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Notifications

The morning reminder is scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

## No tracking, no ads

MorningFacts does not show ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell data.

## Children

MorningFacts is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
