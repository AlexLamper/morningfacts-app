# MorningFacts Support

Need help with MorningFacts? Email **devlamper06@gmail.com** and you'll get a reply within 2 working days.

## Common questions

**How do I restore Premium on a new phone?**
Open the Premium screen and tap **Restore**. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
On iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > MorningFacts > Cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period.

**How do I change my topics or level?**
Open the Topics tab in the app.

**The morning reminder doesn't show up.**
Check Settings > Notifications > MorningFacts and allow notifications.
